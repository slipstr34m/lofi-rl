# Attribution

Credits for every sound source the renderer may load (`assets/MANIFEST.yaml`). Only GeneralUser GS is committed to
this repository. Every other source is fetch-only: it lives in `assets/big/` on the laptop and in the cluster's KEEP
directory, and is never committed or redistributed. Where a source is converted from SFZ to SF2 for FluidSynth, the
SF2 is an adaptation of it and keeps its licence.

  Samples from the "Sax ... single notes" packs by MTG, the Music Technology Group of Universitat Pompeu Fabra,
